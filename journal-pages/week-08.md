# Activity 2 - Critical Design Propositions

**Overview**

*Since I was unable to share my work with a different partner, we decided to exchange feedback on each other's ideas and project development. We then created design propositions based on our discussions, which helped us explore alternative directions and identify areas for improvement in each other's work.*

**Partner's Work Critique**

*What aspect of their approach is most interesting?*

*Their approach to recording their meal intake and the types of food they consume throughout the day is quite interesting. For people with busy lifestyles, it can be difficult to maintain regular eating habits, which makes this an interesting project to explore. It provides insight into how healthy their meal intake is and how it relates to different times of the day or week.*

*What is least developed?*

*The least developed part of the project is the structure of the model and how it will communicate the relationship between the food consumed and the days and times.*

*Is there an alternative form (e.g. physical, screen-based, interactive) that could strengthen the work?*

*An alternative to to strengthen this project could be an interactive digital display. This would would allow the audience to view the patterns between the meals more easily and clearly.*

*How might their future scenario or intended impact be communicated more powerfully?*

*To communicate the intended impact more powerfully, the project could show the relationship between lower intake days, higher intake days and energy levels. This makes it easier for audience to understand how eating habits affects our overall.* 

*What else would you change or do differently if this were your project?*

*If this was my project, I would explore different ways to visualise the data so that interactive elements are easier to understand. The current project uses laser-cut shapes to represent a variety of ingerdients in their meals. Instead, I would group foods into categories and represent them using different geometric shapes, colour or size. This would make the data collection process easier while still allowing the important patterns to be communicated clearly. It would also make the final artefact easier for the audience to understand.*

**Design Proposition**

*My design proposition is to alter the thickness of the strings to categorise foods into healthy, unhealthy, and neutral groups. The scale of the wooden pieces could represent the quantity of food consumed, while different colours could be used to indicate energy levels after each meal. This allows multiple information to be represented within the same model without changing the overall structure.*
 

# Independant Study

**Reflective Summary** 

*The most significant feedback I received was that my project appeared to focus more on the recycled PLA material and the final appearance of the artefact than on the data itself. While the labels explained what the data represented, there was not a strong enough connection between the dataset, the visualisation, and the overall message of the project. The feedback highlighted the importance of finding ways for the audience to interpret the data visually rather than relying on text explanations to understand it. In response to this feedback, I have decided to focus on developing a clearer system for communicating and visualising the data. Rather than using random forms that will mainly represent the final aesthetic of the model, I will explore ways for the size, placement, and arrangement of structures to directly represent the collected PLA waste. I will also look into how different visual elements and patterns can be used to show relationships within the dataset and tell a story to my audience. Making sure to avoid visual complexity and and focus on the main elements of the model that will help understanding the future vision and dataset of this piece. These decisions will shape my project going forward by shifting greater attention towards data communication and visual storytelling. My goal is to ensure that viewers can understand the dataset and its significance through the artefact itself to our world, without needing too many explanations. This will strengthen the connection between the collected PLA waste, the environmental message, and the future scenario being represented. Moving forward, I will continue refining the model and testing different methods of visualising the data to create a final outcome that is both meaningful and easy to interpret.*
