 ## Activity 1 - Concept Sketches

`![Alt text](/assets/week-07/conceptsketch.jpg)'
*In the first activity, we started by developing our initial concept sketches further for the project using drawings and annotations.*

*As seen in the image above, my drawing shows different types of materials collected and placed on a board. The drawing contains labels for each component in the piece. Developing the sketch further allowed me to begin considering how viewers may interact with it.*

*The image was presented in class for the critique session. By providing feedback to my peers, it helped me reflect more critically on my own project and identify areas where my data representation could be clearer. The critique session highlighted the importance of visually communacting something to an audience who are unfamiliar with my project.* 

**What surprised you**

*I recieved many comments on scale of data and artefact, this has quite surprised me as I had not fully planned for this yet. It helped me reconsider the challenge of the data collecting process alongside the scale of the artefact that would influence my audience and encourage their engagement.*

**What aligned with your ideas?**

*I received  praise for the functionality of the piece. This is something I also aimed for throughout the process, creating a piece that other students within the design space can interact with. This also shows that I was able to effectively communicate the interactive aspect of my piece.*

**What you want to follow up on?**

*Many comments I received were unclear about the collection of materials, including how the material would be collected, measured, and used to represent data. This is something I would like to follow up on to make the data representation clearer within the project. In the next stage I would like to explore how quantity and scale can be used to visually communicate my datasets.*

**Reflection**

*Through discussions with my tutor during class, as well as feedback and help from peers, I decided to shift my direction and narrow the dataset I am looking at. I had decided to collect a single material dataset from the design lab. This would help my project become more focused and strengthen the message I would like to communicate to my audience.* 

*I needed to effeciently revise my initial ideas and create a new plan. With thorough consideration, I decided to focus my data collection on PLA filament from the PLA recycling bins within the design studio. This will be the best option for data collection because the material is available within the space, there is a designated area for unused waste of PLA filament meant for recycling and finally I am able to inquire further details about the material from the techniqians which would help me learn more about my data.*

*This data will be collected over the course of three weeks. Collecting PLA filament for recycling is important because it is one of the materials widely used within the design space. There are a few limitations and gaps within this data collection process. The first is that students may not always dispose of their materials in the PLA recycling bins, meaning a large amount of data may not be recorded as it would have ended up in general waste bins. When students do not dipose of their materials in the designated areas many of the data will not be recorded. The second limitation is when some materials intended for filament recycling may have already been recycled before collection if they are not collected and recorded at the right time. These limitations matter because they affect the reliability and accuracy of my dataset.*


## Activity 2 - Making Sprint

`![Alt text](/assets/week-07/makingsprint.jpg)'
 
*Since I was unable to complete the making sprint activity during class because I needed additional time to develop and refine my ideas, I decided to create a rough paper model Once I had a clear idea of what I wanted the project's final artefact to look like. Rather than creating artpiece, I decided to develop the model as a representation of a future environment where materials are reused instead of wasted. The model visualises a sustainable space in which recycled PLA filament is transformed into useful structures and object, demonstrating the value of materials that get wasted on a daily basis.*

*Vision for final artefact*

*The paper model includes a landscape made up of different recycled forms, with the size and volume of each structure representing the amount of PLA waste collected over time. Larger shapes represent greater amounts of collected material, while smaller shapes represent lower amounts. The collection date of the materials will also be represented through different arrangements within the model. The model should include structures and figures to represent a realistic environmental setting and better communicate the impact of material reuse.*
